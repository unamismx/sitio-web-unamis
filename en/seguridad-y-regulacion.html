Safety, ethics and regulation.

Clinical studies are reviewed before and during conduct to protect participants and ensure that research is scientifically justified.

Who reviews a clinical study?

Ethics and research committees

Review the scientific, ethical and regulatory aspects of the protocol and informed consent.

CONBIOÉTICA

In Mexico, the National Bioethics Commission supports standards and the oversight framework for ethics committees.

COFEPRIS

Mexico’s health authority evaluates and authorizes applicable research protocols and amendments.

FDA

The United States Food and Drug Administration reviews studies and products within U.S. regulatory pathways.

EMA

The European Medicines Agency coordinates scientific evaluation for medicines in the European Union.

Informed consent

Before participation, the team explains the study, alternatives, risks, benefits and the right to withdraw.
