Research team

Clinical expertise and dedicated operations.

UNAMIS brings together associated investigators, sub-investigators and an operational team experienced in multiple therapeutic areas.

Investigator network

Specialists and sub-investigators in rheumatology, oncology, gastroenterology, pediatrics, dermatology, nephrology, infectious diseases and endocrinology.

Clinical operations

Coordination structure dedicated to protocol conduct, participant care, documentation and operational milestones.

Specialized support

Clinical, regulatory, pharmacy, laboratory and data-management resources assigned according to protocol needs.

Training and continuity

A multidisciplinary structure for each protocol.

The team receives training in Good Clinical Practice, cold-chain management, cardiopulmonary resuscitation and basic or advanced life support according to role. Training documentation and professional credentials are maintained for feasibility and site-selection processes.
